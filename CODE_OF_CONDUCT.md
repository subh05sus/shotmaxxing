# Code of conduct

Shotmaxxing is built by people with different backgrounds, experience and opinions. We want taking part to be a good
experience for everyone, whatever their age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## What we expect

- Be kind and assume good intent. Most disagreements are misunderstandings.
- Critique work, not people. Explain the problem and, where you can, suggest a better way.
- Accept feedback gracefully, and give credit generously.
- Help newcomers find their way; everyone started somewhere.

## What isn't acceptable

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks, in public or in private.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Trolling, or deliberately derailing discussions.
- Anything else a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour worries you, report it privately to the maintainers through
[GitHub's private reporting](https://github.com/subh05sus/shotmaxxing/security/advisories/new) (mark the report as a
conduct issue) or by a direct message to the repository owner. Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers may remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions that break this code, and may ban people
temporarily or permanently for repeated or serious breaches.

## Scope

This code applies in every project space (issues, pull requests, discussions, chat) and when someone represents the
project in public.
