# Captions

Shotmaxxing writes down what you say and times it to the video, in 99 languages. It runs on this computer with
Whisper, so nothing is uploaded. Press <kbd>7</kbd> for the AI tool.

## Make captions

1. Choose **Generate captions**. The first time, it downloads the speech model (about 60 MB), only this once, and
   keeps it on this computer.
2. Wait a moment while it listens. You can keep working, or **Cancel**.
3. The lines appear in the tool, each with its time.

Captions are made from your voice. If the take has none, record a voice-over first. See
[Sound](sound.md#record-a-voice-over).

## Fix them

- Click a line's time to jump there.
- Edit any word in place. Clear a line to remove it.
- **Make again** listens again from the start, and **Remove** takes all the captions away.

## Show them in the video or save them

- **Show captions** draws them into the exported video. **Caption style** is **Bar** or **Bold**.
- **Save subtitles** writes an **SRT** or **WebVTT** file, for YouTube and other players that show their own
  subtitles.

> [!NOTE]
> The model comes from Hugging Face and is checked against a pinned hash before it's used. See [Privacy](privacy.md).
