# Background and shape

The Background tool puts the recording on a wallpaper, with padding, rounded corners and a shadow, and sets the shape
of the whole video. Press <kbd>1</kbd> to open it.

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Wallpaper | The picture or colour behind the recording |
| Padding | The space between the recording and the edge of the video |
| Corner radius | How round the recording's corners are |
| Shadow | How strong the shadow under the recording is |
| Aspect ratio | 16:9, 4:3, 1:1 or 9:16. Screenshots also have **Fit**, their own shape |

## Pick a shape for where it's going

| Going to | Shape |
| --- | --- |
| YouTube, a website, a slide | 16:9 |
| X, LinkedIn | 1:1 |
| Shorts, Reels, TikTok | 9:16 |

The [export](export.md) presets choose a shape too. When a preset's shape differs from the project's, the export sheet
offers to use the preset's shape.

## Looks from content packs

A content pack can add looks: a wallpaper, padding, corners and shadow chosen together, shown at the top of the tool.
Pick one to apply it all at once. See [Content packs](../content-packs.md).
