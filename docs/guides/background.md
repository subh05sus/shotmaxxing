# Background and shape

The Background tool puts the recording on a background, with padding, rounded corners and a shadow, and sets the
shape of the whole video. Press <kbd>1</kbd> to open it.

## What goes behind it

Pick a tab, and its background goes on at once:

| Tab | What's in it |
| --- | --- |
| **Image** | 20 photos and abstract pictures that come with the app, and **Upload custom** for your own PNG, JPEG or WebP. Your picture is copied into the project, so it travels with it |
| **Color** | Any solid colour, from the picker, a hex code or the swatches |
| **Gradient** | The gradient wallpapers (and any from content packs), plus **Make your own** from two colours and an angle |

**Background blur** softens whatever is behind the recording, so a busy photo doesn't pull the eye from the demo. It
sits right under the tabs and works on every kind except a single colour, which looks the same blurred.

## The frame

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Padding | The space between the recording and the edge of the video |
| Corner radius | How round the recording's corners are |
| Shadow | How strong the shadow under the recording is |
| Aspect ratio | 16:9, 4:3, 1:1 or 9:16. Screenshots also have **Fit**, their own shape |

## Pick a shape for where it's going

| Going to | Shape |
| --- | --- |
| YouTube, a website, a slide | 16:9 |
| X, LinkedIn | 1:1 |
| Shorts, Reels, TikTok | 9:16 |

The [export](export.md) presets choose a shape too. When a preset's shape differs from the project's, the export sheet
offers to use the preset's shape.

## Looks from content packs

A content pack can add looks: a wallpaper, padding, corners and shadow chosen together, shown at the top of the tool.
Pick one to apply it all at once. See [Content packs](../content-packs.md).
