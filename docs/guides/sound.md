# Sound, voice-over and music

The Audio tool sets the levels of your voice and the computer's sound, cleans up your voice, cuts long silences,
records a voice-over and adds music. Press <kbd>6</kbd> to open it.

## Levels

**Microphone** and **System audio** each have their own volume. System audio is the computer's sound, recorded when
you turned it on in the record dialog.

## Clean up

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Clean up my voice | Turns down hum, fan noise and hiss |
| Cut long silences | Pauses over a second where nothing happens on screen become short ones |

Both run on this computer. Nothing is uploaded.

## Record a voice-over

1. Move the playhead to where you want to start talking.
2. Choose **Record a voice-over**, and talk while the video plays. It plays without sound, so nothing echoes into
   your microphone.
3. Press **Stop** (or <kbd>Space</kbd>) when you're done, or **Discard** to throw it away.

Each voice-over shows where it starts and how long it is, with its own **Volume**. Choose **Record another** to add
more, or remove one with **×**.

A voice-over can be captioned like any other voice. See [Captions](captions.md).

## Add music

1. Choose **Add music…** and pick a sound file.
2. It plays under the whole video, straight through cuts, and fades out at the end.
3. Set its volume, choose **Choose other music…** to swap it, or remove it with **×**.
