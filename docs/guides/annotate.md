# Titles, arrows, blurs and keystrokes

Annotate adds things on top of the recording: a title, an arrow pointing at something, or a blur over what you don't
want to share. Press <kbd>4</kbd> to open it (<kbd>2</kbd> for a screenshot).

## Add one

1. Move the playhead to where it should appear.
2. Choose **Title**, **Arrow** or **Blur**. It's added at the playhead.
3. Drag it into place on the picture, or nudge it with the arrow keys.
4. Drag its block on the timeline to change when it shows and for how long.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Blur emails, tokens and names before you share a video. A blur is drawn into the exported video, so nobody can
> take it off.

## Show keystrokes

**Show keystrokes** draws the keys you press on screen, such as <kbd>⌘</kbd><kbd>K</kbd>, so viewers can follow
along. Typing in password fields never shows.
